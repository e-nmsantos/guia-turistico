An interactive, multilingual Python web application designed for professional **Tour Guides**, **Heritage Interpreters**, and **Tourism Students**. Fully aligned with the official national tour guiding certification syllabus, covering **Professional English**, **Spanish**, and **Technical Portuguese**.

---

## 📌 Features Overview

- 🌐 **100% Trilingual Curriculum:** Switch seamlessly between **English**, **Spanish**, and **Technical Portuguese** across all modules and lessons.
- 🔊 **Integrated Native Speech Synthesis:** Listen to natural pronunciation for every technical term, historical name, and field speech script in `en-GB`, `es-ES`, and `pt-PT`.
- 🏰 **Monument Spotlight (Belém Tower):** Complete architectural and military analysis of UNESCO-listed *Torre de Belém* (1514–1519), featuring audio-guided tour scripts and field Q&A management.
- 📇 **Searchable Technical Glossary:** Comprehensive dictionary spanning crowd control, enology, architectural styles, blazonry, and diplomatic treaties.
- 🃏 **Interactive Flashcards:** Rapid-fire memorization mode with answer reveal and audio pronunciation.
- 🎯 **Official Certification Quizzes:** Multiple-choice exams across all 5 modules with instant scoring and detailed technical explanations.
- 🎙️ **Guiding Studio & Roleplay Scenarios:** Step-by-step real-world tour delivery scripts for Belém, Douro/Port Wine Lodges, and Sintra's Romantic Palaces.

## 📚 Syllabus Modules Covered

### 1. General English & Guiding Communication Skills
- **1.1 Pronunciation & Vocal Delivery:** Diaphragmatic voice projection, acoustic management in outdoor settings, and IPA comparative guide for Portuguese toponyms.
- **1.2 Functional Grammar & Professional Etiquette:** Diplomatic modal phrasing, formal address, and de-escalation formulas.
- **1.3 Operational Vocabulary:** Crowd flow management, cobblestone safety warnings, fast-track ticketing, and emergency protocols.
- **1.4 Narrative Techniques & Storytelling:** The *Hook-Narrative-Meaning* framework and the *L.E.A.R.N.* conflict resolution method.

### 2. Gastronomy & Wine Heritage
- **2.1 Demarcated Wine Regions (DOC):** The 1756 Douro demarcated region, Vinho Verde, Dão, Alentejo, Colares ungrafted vines, Madeira, and Port wine fortification (*benefício*, Ruby vs. Tawny).
- **2.2 Traditional Menus & Allergen Alerts:** Technical descriptions of iconic dishes (*Bacalhau à Brás*, *Cataplana*, *Francesinha*), convent confectionery, and EU allergen regulations.
- **2.3 Cross-Cultural Culinary Exchanges:** Portuguese origins of Japanese *Tempura* (*peixinhos da horta*), Goan *Vindaloo*, and Catherine of Braganza's introduction of afternoon tea to the British Royal Court (1662).

### 3. Architecture & Heritage
- **3.1 Sacred Architecture:** Chronological evolution from Fortress-Romanesque and Cistercian Gothic to triumphant Maritime Manueline and 18th-century Baroque gilded woodcarving (*Talha Dourada*).
- **3.2 Palaces & Civil Heritage:** 19th-century Romanticism at Pena Palace (Sintra), Queluz National Palace (the Portuguese Versailles), and Mafra Palace-Monastery.
- **3.3 Museums & Masterpieces:** National Coach Museum, National Tile Museum (*Great Lisbon Panorama*), and Ancient Art Museum (MNAA).

### 4. Introduction to Heraldry
- **4.1 Civic Municipal Heraldry (1930 Code):** Mural crowns system (5 golden towers for Lisbon, 5 silver for Cities, 4 silver for Towns, 3 for Parishes).
- **4.2 Blazonry & National Arms:** The 5 Blue Shields (*Quinas*), 7 Golden Castles, King Manuel I's Armillary Sphere, heraldic tinctures (*Or, Argent, Gules, Azure, Vert, Sable*), and the Rule of Contrast.

### 5. History of Portugal & European Links
- **5.1 Pre-History to 1128:** Foundation of Portugal at the Battle of São Mamede (1128) vs. the 1066 Norman Conquest of England; role of English crusaders in the 1147 Siege of Lisbon.
- **5.2 Monarchy until 1910:** The 1386 Treaty of Windsor (world's oldest active alliance), the *Illustrious Generation*, the 1703 Methuen Treaty, and the Duke of Wellington's Lines of Torres Vedras during the Peninsular War.
- **5.3 1910 to Present:** Proclamation of the Republic (1910), neutral Lisbon as a WWII spy hub and refugee haven (Ian Fleming & James Bond at Estoril), the 1974 Carnation Revolution, and EU integration.

## 🚀 Quick Start (Local Run)

### Option 1: One-Click Launcher (Windows)
Double-click:
```bat
run.bat
```

### Option 2: Command Line
1. Clone the repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME/guia-turistico-pro.git
   cd guia-turistico-pro
   ```
2. Install dependencies:
   ```bash
   pip install -r requirements.txt
   ```
3. Run the Streamlit application:
   ```bash
   streamlit run app.py
   ```
The app will open automatically in your browser at `http://localhost:8501`.
